Based on the provided semantic summaries for the Hadoop YARN Capacity Scheduler package, here is a comprehensive architectural recovery report addressing all requested sections:

### 1. **Module Title**
**Capacity Scheduler with Preemption Management for Hadoop YARN**

### 2. **Architectural Purpose**
The primary purpose of this module is to manage resource allocation efficiently within a Hadoop YARN cluster, incorporating a capacity scheduler that supports preemption. This enables dynamic adjustment of resources based on priority, availability, and current workload demands.

### 3. **Main Responsibilities**
- **Resource Management**: Dynamically allocates and deallocates resources across queues.
- **Preemption Handling**: Identifies and acts upon opportunities to preempt lower-priority containers to accommodate higher-priority workloads.
- **Queue Management**: Maintains a hierarchical queue structure, ensuring resource allocations align with defined capacities and priorities.

### 4. **Component Interactions**
- **PreemptableContainer**: Provides encapsulated access to containers marked for preemption, interacting with the **Preemption Manager** for lifecycle management.
- **PreemptableQueue**: Manages resources and containers within a queue, notifying the **Preemption Manager** of changes and utilizing **Capacity Scheduler** for allocation decisions.
- **Preemption Manager**: Orchestrates preemptable resources and containers across queues, collaborating with **Capacity Scheduler** for informed preemption decisions.
- **Capacity Scheduler**: Allocates resources based on queue capacities, priorities, and preemption needs, integrating feedback from **Preemption Manager**.

### 5. **Scheduling Behavior**
- **Priority-Based Scheduling**: Allocates resources prioritizing queues and containers based on predefined settings.
- **Dynamic Preemption**: Continuously assesses the need for preemption, adjusting resource allocations to meet high-priority demands.
- **Capacity Constrained**: Ensures allocations do not exceed defined queue capacities, preventing overcommitment.

### 6. **Resource Allocation Behavior**
- **Initial Allocation**: Assigns resources to newly submitted containers based on available capacity and priority.
- **Rebalancing**: Periodically reviews and adjusts allocations to optimize utilization, adherence to queue capacities, and response to preemption requests.
- **Deallocation**: Efficiently reclaims resources from completed or preempted containers, making them available for new allocations.

### 7. **Queue Management Logic**
- **Hierarchy Maintenance**: Ensures the queue structure reflects the current state of the YARN cluster, including updates due to preemption actions.
- **Capacity Enforcement**: Strictly adheres to defined queue capacities during resource allocations, preventing overallocation.
- **Priority Resolution**: Resolves conflicts by prioritizing queues and containers based on configured settings and real-time demand.

### 8. **Preemption Logic (if applicable)**
- **Eligibility Assessment**: Identifies containers eligible for preemption based on priority, resource usage, and queue capacity constraints.
- **Impact Minimization**: Selects containers for preemption that minimize disruption to ongoing workloads while satisfying high-priority resource needs.
- **Graceful Termination**: Ensures preempted containers are terminated gracefully, preserving data integrity and minimizing recovery overhead.

### 9. **Important Dependencies**
- **YARN Resource Manager**: Collaborates for global resource visibility and state synchronization.
- **YARN Scheduler Components**: Integrates with other scheduler modules for holistic cluster management.
- **Hadoop Ecosystem**: Leverages Hadoop’s resource management and scheduling APIs for seamless integration.

### 10. **Design Patterns**
- **Observer Pattern**: Employed in the interaction between **Preemption Manager** and **PreemptableQueue**, where the manager observes queue changes.
- **Facade Pattern**: Used by the **Capacity Scheduler** to simplify complex resource allocation logic, providing a unified interface.
- **Hierarchical Structure**: Implemented in queue management, allowing for scalable and organized resource allocation.

### 11. **Distributed Systems Observations**
- **Scalability**: Designed to scale with the YARN cluster, managing increased complexity through hierarchical queue structures.
- **Fault Tolerance**: Incorporates mechanisms (e.g., graceful termination) to mitigate the impact of failures on overall system reliability.
- **Performance Optimization**: Continuously monitors and adjusts resource allocations to maximize throughput and minimize latency, responding to dynamic workload conditions.
