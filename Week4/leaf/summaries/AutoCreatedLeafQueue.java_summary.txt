**Overview**
----------------

The `AutoCreatedLeafQueue` component, part of the YARN Resource Manager's Capacity Scheduler subsystem, resides within the Business Logic Layer. It is responsible for managing auto-created leaf queues within a hierarchical queue structure, ensuring seamless integration with parent queues and cluster resource management.

**Key Responsibilities**
-----------------------------

* **Queue Management**: Handles auto-created leaf queue reinitialization, capacity updates, and validation.
* **Core Operations**:
	+ Reinitializes queue configurations based on newly parsed `CSQueue` objects.
	+ Merges capacity settings from `AutoCreatedLeafQueueConfig` templates with existing configurations.
	+ Validates capacity demands to ensure they fall within the valid range (0 to 1).
* **Integration Points**:
	+ Communicates with `ManagedParentQueue` for inheriting and updating configurations.
	+ Interacts with the `QueueManager` for node label management.
	+ Utilizes cluster resource information for capacity calculations and validations.

**Input/Output and Error Handling**

	- Cluster resource information
* **Outputs**:
