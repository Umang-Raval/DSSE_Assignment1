----------------

The `CapacitySchedulerConfigValidator` component, situated within the Resource Management layer of the Hadoop YARN framework, is responsible for meticulously validating configurations destined for the Capacity Scheduler. This crucial process ensures the correctness, consistency, and backward compatibility of scheduler settings, encompassing memory and VCore allocations, intricate queue hierarchies, and placement rules.
